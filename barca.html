Welcome to Barcelona

Barcelona is one of Spain's most exciting destinations, combining beautiful beaches, impressive architecture, rich history and unforgettable food. From the famous Sagrada Família to the lively streets of the Gothic Quarter, there is something for everyone to discover. As part of the El Dorado cruise, Barcelona offers the perfect opportunity to explore Mediterranean culture and experience a truly memorable destination.

Discover the City

Barcelona Highlights

Sagrada Família

Visit Barcelona's world-famous basilica, designed by Antoni Gaudí. Its extraordinary architecture makes it one of the city's most recognisable landmarks.

Gothic Quarter

Explore historic streets, charming squares and traditional Spanish architecture in the heart of Barcelona.

Barceloneta Beach

Enjoy the Mediterranean coastline, relax by the sea and experience the atmosphere of Barcelona's beaches.

The Barcelona Experience

More Than Just a Destination

Barcelona is a city full of energy, creativity and tradition. Visitors can experience its Mediterranean lifestyle while discovering its famous landmarks and local culture.
