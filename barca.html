Barcelona

A city full of culture and beauty

Discover Barcelona

Barcelona is the second destination on our El Dorado cruise. Located in Spain, it is a beautiful city known for its architecture, beaches, culture and history. One of its most famous attractions is the Sagrada Família, designed by Antoni Gaudí. Visitors can also explore the Gothic Quarter, walk along Las Ramblas and enjoy the Mediterranean coastline.

What makes Barcelona special?

Barcelona has something for everyone. Visitors can discover its famous landmarks, enjoy traditional Spanish food and experience the lively atmosphere of the city.

  • Sagrada Família – Barcelona's famous basilica.
  • Park Güell – A colourful park designed by Gaudí.
  • Las Ramblas – A popular street full of shops and cafés.
  • Barceloneta Beach – A beautiful Mediterranean beach.
