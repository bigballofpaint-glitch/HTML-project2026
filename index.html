About the Cruise

El Dorado is a cruise around Spain, stopping in Barcelona, Palma de Mallorca and Alicante. Along the way, passengers can explore different cities, enjoy the views and take part in activities on board.

Onboard Facilities

  • Swimming pool
  • Restaurants and cafes
  • Gym and spa
  • Outdoor sun deck
  • Private cabins

Onboard Activities

  • Live music
  • Movie nights
  • Yoga classes
  • Poolside games
  • Evening entertainment
