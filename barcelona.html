Barcelona

Welcome to Barcelona

Barcelona is a beautiful city in Spain, famous for its architecture, beaches, food and culture. It is one of the most exciting destinations on our El Dorado Mediterranean cruise.

Famous Attractions

Sagrada Família is one of Barcelona's most famous landmarks, designed by Antoni Gaudí. Visitors can also explore Park Güell, Las Ramblas and Barceloneta Beach.

Culture and Lifestyle

Barcelona offers delicious Spanish food, historic streets, beautiful buildings and a lively atmosphere. It is the perfect place to experience Mediterranean culture.
