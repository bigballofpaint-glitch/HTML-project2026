Palma

Where history meets the sea

Discover Mallorca's capital

Palma, the capital of Mallorca, is a Mediterranean city known for its golden stone buildings, lively waterfront and beautiful historic streets. One of its most famous landmarks is La Seu, the impressive Gothic cathedral overlooking the sea. Visitors can explore the old town's narrow lanes, relax beside the harbour and enjoy the cafés, shops and local food that give the city its welcoming atmosphere.

A taste of Majorcan music
