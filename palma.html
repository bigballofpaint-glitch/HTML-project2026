Palma

A mediterranean paradise

Discover Mallorca's capital

Our cruise's second destination is Palma, the capital of Mallorca, it is a city known for its Spanish/Moorish heritage and beautiful architecture. One of its most famous landmarks is 'La Seu', the impressive Gothic cathedral right on the sea. Tourists can explore the old town's pretty streets, relax beside the becach and enjoy the cafés, shops and local food that give the city its welcoming atmosphere.

The trad music of Palma
